# Communication Coach question matrix

This is the role-specific base question in the app. Each question has five difficulty versions. The independent retry changes the situation so the learner has to apply the skill again.

## Human Resources

| Category | Goal | Situation | First question | Independent retry |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Daily practice | Explain ideas clearly | A new employee asks how to request time off during their first week. | Explain the request process, who approves it, and where they can find the policy. What would you say? | The employee has an urgent exception. Explain the next contact and what you can confirm today. |
| Professional practice | Give constructive feedback | A manager asks you to support a performance improvement plan with incomplete examples. | Explain what evidence is missing, why it matters for fairness, and the next step you recommend. | The manager wants to start tomorrow. State a responsible interim step and a date for the evidence review. |
| Leadership practice | Present recommendations | Leaders want to change a people policy that will affect several teams. | Present the decision needed, the employee impact, the evidence and uncertainty, and a communication plan. | A leader challenges the impact estimate. Explain the assumption and a way to test it before rollout. |

## Engineering

|---|---|---|---|---|

## Five badge levels

The badge is based on completed practice evidence. A missed day does not erase a level: the best completed-practice streak is used for the badge, while the current streak is shown separately. All requirements for a level must be met.

| Level | Badge | Practice days | Best streak | Distinct scenarios | Communication goals | Successful independent retries | Retry assessments |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 🐣 Novice | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| 2 | 🐦 Beginner | 2 | 2 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| 3 | 🦜 Intermediate | 5 | 4 | 3 | 2 | 2 | 2 |
| 4 | 🦉 Professional | 10 | 7 | 6 | 3 | 4 | 4 |
| 5 | 🦅 Mastery | 20 | 14 | 10 | 4 | 7 | 7 |

The badge tracks practice progression. AI scores appear only after a grounded AI review; the app does not invent a score when assessment is unavailable.

## Gamification

Earned XP is cumulative: 20 per distinct completed scenario, 5 per distinct completed-practice day, 5 per day in the best completed-practice streak, and 15 per distinct scenario with a successful independent retry. Repeating the same scenario does not award its scenario or retry XP again. The weekly challenge counts completed practices in the last seven days toward a target of three.

Achievements unlock for the first completed practice, a two-day streak, three communication goals, successful retries in three distinct scenarios, and ten practice days. Locked achievements remain visible as goals. XP and badges reflect practice activity; AI skill scores are shown separately only when a grounded review exists.
